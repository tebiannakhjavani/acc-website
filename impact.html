Our first year

ACC spent its first year fundraising and building the club — most of what we raised was saved to fund larger donations ahead. Our first partnership, with GAIA, put around ₪1,000–₪2,000 worth of toys and supplies into the hands of refugee children through a Christmas toy drive.

How It Works

1. Fundraise

We plan and run fundraising events at school.

2. Partner

We choose a charity in Israel and agree where the money will go.

3. Donate

We donate the proceeds and share the result with the school.
