Charities We Support

We partner with charities in Israel and agree with each one where our donation will go before we fundraise for it.

GAIA

Our first charity partner. ACC worked with GAIA on a Christmas toy drive: our students bought toys and supplies worth around ₪1,000–₪2,000 and gave them to refugee children.

Krembo Wings

Krembo Wings (Knafaim Shel Krembo) is an Israeli youth movement that brings together children and young people with and without disabilities. ACC donated to support its work with young people across Israel.

Building Our Fund

ACC's first year was about fundraising and setting the club up properly. Most of what we raised was saved, building a fund for larger donations as the club grows.

Are you a charity?

If you are a charity in Israel and would like to work with a student-led club, get in touch — we are always looking for our next partner.
