About Us

About ACC

Founded by seniors, carried on by students — a school charity club built to keep giving back, year after year.

Our story

ACC was founded in 2025 by seniors at AIS who wanted students to do more than talk about helping — they wanted to organise it. When they graduated, the club was handed over to the current team, who have carried it on since.

We run fundraisers at school, partner with charities in Israel, and donate what we raise where it can do the most good. Every event is planned, promoted, and run by students. Our first partnership, with GAIA, saw students buy toys and supplies for refugee children as part of a Christmas toy drive.

Student-Led

Every fundraiser is organised by students, from the first idea to the final donation.

Community First

We bring the whole school together around causes that matter locally.

Real Impact

Money raised at school becomes a real donation to a real charity in Israel.

The handover

ACC started with the class of 2026. Today it is run by a new team of student leaders — meet them on the Team page, and see what the club has done on the Impact page.
